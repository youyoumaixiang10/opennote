# How to Make Amazing Videos with Claude and HyperFrames (Peter Yang × Jake Moran)

**Link**: https://youtu.be/MC1g2fHYC88
**Domain**: AI, video creation, content creation tools
**Relevance**: A hands-on workflow for using Claude (Opus / Claude Code) with HyperFrames, HeyGen's free, open-source tool that renders HTML into video. It is useful for making launch videos and social content without a traditional editor.
**Summary**: Jake Moran shows Peter Yang how he prompts Claude to make and edit videos with HyperFrames. He covers the editing words that get Claude to do what you want, building a launch video from three screenshots, copying a favorite creator's video style, and giving feedback by drawing on the frame. He also covers polishing long-form videos and shorts.

## Key points (source: Peter Yang's X post and episode page)

- "Anyone can now make amazing videos with Claude Opus if you know how to prompt it."
- Make a launch video from just a few images (Jake started with 3 screenshots: one of the studio, and the rest for the hook)
- Duplicate a favorite creator's video style
- Make precise edits, including giving feedback by drawing directly on the frame
- At one point, Claude added Jake's AI avatar without being asked
