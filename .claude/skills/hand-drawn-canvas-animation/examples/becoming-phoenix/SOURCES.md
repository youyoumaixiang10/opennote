# Sources

The character drawings build on [the sketchbook bird study](../sketchbook-bird.html).
The additional drawings, environments, animation and synthesized music are
included as source. The engines are shared from `../../assets/`.

- Lantern photograph: The Metropolitan Museum of Art, Lantern, 1825-31,
  [collection record](https://www.metmuseum.org/art/collection/search/16306),
  Open Access (CC0). The embedded cutout in `photos.js` is reused from
  [night-shift-photos.js](../night-shift-photos.js).
- X logo: [official brand toolkit](https://about.x.com/en/who-we-are/brand-toolkit).
  Its path and the creator's handle appear in the example's closing card.
  They are example branding, not defaults for new films.

No external music recordings or video-generation models are used.
