# The reference films

Five films by Kevin Ngo define the five looks. The notes below are
measurements of the published videos, not claims from the posts. Use them to
match a look closely. Do not copy the films; make your own subject.

| film | post | length | container | drawn cadence | cuts | look |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| The life of a fruit fly | [2099858454043349342](https://x.com/kevin_t_ngo/status/2099858454043349342), 15 Sep 2026 | 27.0 s | 1080², 24 fps, 648 frames | 12 fps on twos: even frames are duplicates (median diff 0.74 vs 4.37 on odd pairs; 101 of 104 frozen frames on even positions) | 10 hard cuts, two flicker runs | ink |
| Flipbook (untitled) | [2099477219877978289](https://x.com/kevin_t_ngo/status/2099477219877978289), 14 Sep 2026 | 28.0 s | 1080², 24 fps, 672 frames | mostly on twos (even 5.3 vs odd 8.0), montage on threes | one cut every 6 frames from 6.5 to 13.9 s (about 30 cards) | riso |
| Paper boat (untitled) | [2099308402887520495](https://x.com/kevin_t_ngo/status/2099308402887520495), 14 Sep 2026 | 26.0 s | 1080², 24 fps, 624 frames | held frames dominate (250 of 624 near-frozen), motion on twos | ~30 cuts, most 0.25 to 1 s apart between 8.75 and 18 s | screen |
| Personal website | [2093500169723814093](https://x.com/kevin_t_ngo/status/2093500169723814093), 29 Aug 2026 | 51.5 s | 1080², 30 fps, 1545 frames | a screen recording of a page scrolling: no cadence, no cuts | none; sections divided by torn edges | pencil |
| Doodles on photos (untitled) | [2100601972902842517](https://x.com/kevin_t_ngo/status/2100601972902842517), 17 Sep 2026 | 34.0 s | 1080², 24 fps, 816 frames | lines draw on at full 24 fps; drawn characters move in held poses of 2 to 6 frames | 15 hard cuts, one every 1.5 to 2.5 s, plus a 4 s gallery | doodle |

Textures do not boil in any of the first four: on a static shot consecutive frames
differ by about 1/255, which is codec noise. From the author's replies under
the fly post: the film is one HTML file, the music was written in code and
timed to the animation, and slowing it down would lose the animated feel.

## The life of a fruit fly (ink)

Warm paper with diagonal light bands, a peach, a fly built from ellipses with
hex-lattice eyes, hatching along every form, grain, thin blue construction
lines, riso-coloured scribble outlines on the thorax and abdomen. Blueprint
interludes on navy with chalk lines explain the egg, the pupa and the brain.

## Flipbook (riso)

Cream stock with visible paper fibre. A navy seed dot sits at the exact
centre of every frame. Crayon-textured ripples in blue and magenta expand
from it. Then a montage: full-bleed illustrated cards at 4 per second (koi
pond, dragonfly, jellyfish, owl, temple bell, lighthouse, wolf, telephone,
turntable, frog and moon, bats, wave, cat at a window, sunflower and bee,
radio, fireworks, hummingbird, kettle, ferris wheel, waterfall, bicycle,
whale, harp, rocket, city, ringed planet, balloons, volcano, dish antenna,
campfire, sunflower field, mountains, tree, ice). Every card is a riso print:
three or four inks (blue, magenta, yellow, sometimes green or orange), each a
halftone dot screen about 8 px apart at its own angle, overlaps multiplied,
edges misregistered by a few pixels. Then all cards as round badges on
concentric dashed rings, shrinking to the dot. Then a duotone run (magenta
and blue only: waterfall, whale, rocket, planet). Then a purple-navy
starfield with circled dots. Then the sign-off "opus 5 / claude" in navy
with a magenta offset and two dots.

| t, s | shot |
|---|---|
| 0.0 to 4.3 | seed dot, ripples, three iris peeks at cards |
| 4.5 to 13.9 | card montage, 0.25 s per card |
| 14.25 to 15.9 | cards again inside an iris with wobbly rings |
| 16.0 to 17.5 | badge gallery on rings, shrink to the dot |
| 18.0 to 22.9 | ripples in two inks, a second dot, a flower |
| 23.0 to 23.9 | duotone cards |
| 24.0 to 25.9 | starfield with circled dots and dashed rings |
| 26.0 to 28.0 | sign-off |

Measured palette: cream `#f0ece2` 45%, navy `#22366b`, tan `#cdae84`, teal
`#658f8f`, plum `#804371`, pale yellow `#f3ebb1`. A third of the pixels are
saturated.

## Paper boat (screen)

A teal desk with a mesh texture, a sheet of paper, a pencil. Writing appears
on the sheet as squiggle rows; the sheet folds into a boat in four hard cuts.
Then the white boat sits at the same spot while the world changes behind it
about thirty times: a sunny lake with reeds, stones, rain and a rainbow, a
harbour town with red roofs, a grey factory, a night city of yellow windows,
a starry sea, jellyfish, a lighthouse, a big red ship, a wave, a storm with
lightning, a whale, fish, a swimming pool, lily pads, mangroves, ice, a
sunset, a map, a seigaiha wave pattern, islands, a huge moon, a house on a
hill at night. Every flat shape carries a regular dot screen in a darker tone
of itself. Then back to a wooden desk, the boat unfolds into the written
sheet, and the sign-off appears in white on the night sky.

Measured palette: blues `#0a5083`, `#051630`, `#194a74`, teal `#518e9d`,
grey-blue `#becacc`, cream `#e8e6db`, orange `#e4a05c`, olive `#91906a`.

## Personal website (pencil)

Not a frame-by-frame film but a page, recorded while scrolling. Cream paper,
tiny charcoal text, a thin olive thread wandering down the whole page,
circles with an asterisk inside, walls of squiggle text shaped like
mountains, pressed-flower drawings on a pale pink section, a line of sheet
music, sticky notes with spirals, a sage-green section with grass, a quilt
sampler of patterns, a twisted ladder, a figure behind a grid, a warm black
section with dotted concentric arcs, a fish drawn in thin lines, dot
fireworks, a boat with a figure inside ripples, a stamp grid of birds, a
figure inside an enso, and a fade to grey. Sections meet at torn-paper edges.

Measured palette: cream `#f4efe4` 36%, charcoal `#201f1b` 19%, sage
`#e0e2d0` 18%, warm grey `#dad2c5`; saturation 0.09.

## Doodles on photos (doodle)

The post says the model searched the web, downloaded images it liked and
doodled on them, every doodle drawn in JavaScript. Nothing else is published,
so the method in `doodle.md` is reconstructed from the frames.

Fourteen objects, one per shot, each a cut-out photo on its own pastel sheet
with a soft contact shadow and a vignette of about 4% towards the corners:

| t, s | paper | object | becomes |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0.0 | mint `#d3e6d9` | alarm clock | a bed; it rings, the photo shakes, the cat flies off |
| 2.5 | rose `#efd2d1` | enamel cup | a hot bath, cat inside behind the rim, a ladder, a rubber duck |
| 4.5 | butter `#efe4b3` | toaster | a launch pad, the cat rides the toast |
| 6.5 | sky `#d2dee8` | two waffles | a climbing wall with a summit flag |
| 8.0 | cream `#ebe5d4` | shoe | a ship: mast, patched sail, bunting, sea drawn over the sole |
| 10.0 | peach `#eeccb4` | rubber boots | a frog's house in the rain, a flower grows from the other boot |
| 12.0 | lilac `#ded4e9` | twin-lens camera | a family portrait, flash, a print pinned up |
| 13.5 | sky | envelope | cargo: two birds fly it, "to: you" |
| 15.0 | mint | old book | a cliff; a train runs off its top edge into a drawn desert |
| 17.0 | butter | bakelite radio | a dance floor with a disco ball |
| 19.5 | rose | stork scissors | a stork carrying a baby |
| 21.0 | mint | kettle | its steam is a cat-shaped heart, the cat swings on the handle |

| 30.0 | sand `#c5ac7b` | all fourteen as thumbnails | gallery and a handwritten signature |

What repeats in every shot: the photo alone for 2 to 6 frames; a first line
that states the misreading (the mast, the ladder); white-bodied characters so
they read on the photo; watercolour that arrives after the line and sits off
it; one or two handwritten words ("ahh~", "wheee!", "click!", "FWEEE!"); a gag
in the last third where either the drawing or the photo itself moves. Drawings
go behind the object (ground lines), on it (a lifebuoy on the shoe) and inside
it (the cat in the cup is cut by the front rim). One cat, one mouse and one
frog return through the whole film.

## What to take from all of them

- One anchor survives every cut: the fly, the dot, the boat, the thread.
- Every surface carries its finish, and the finish never changes inside a
  shot.
- Cuts are hard and fast; transitions are drawn (blot, iris, ripple, torn
  edge), never blended.
- Each film ends with the same two words in the author's hand, in two inks.
- Music follows the cuts, which is easy only because picture and sound come
  from one timeline.
